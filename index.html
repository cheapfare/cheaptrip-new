How the Booking Process Works

Step 1: Search or Request Assistance

Customers can begin by searching for flights directly on our website by entering their travel details. Alternatively, customers may contact our support team if they require assistance in finding suitable travel options worldwide.

Step 2: Explore and Compare Options

Users can review available flight options based on airline, travel time, route, and pricing. For assisted bookings, our team may guide customers through available choices based on their preferences and travel needs.

Step 3: Select Travel Itinerary

Once a suitable flight option is identified, the customer selects the itinerary they wish to book for their journey.

Step 4: Review Pricing Details

Before proceeding to payment, customers are shown the total cost of the booking. This may include:

  • Base airfare
  • Airline taxes and fees
  • Any applicable service or processing charges

All pricing details are presented before final confirmation.

Step 5: Payment Processing

Payment is securely processed by cheaptriptoday using authorized payment systems. Customers are not redirected to unrelated third-party payment entities.

Step 6: Booking Submission and Processing

After payment is successfully completed, the booking request is submitted to the airline's system for processing. Processing time may vary depending on airline systems, seat availability, and fare conditions.

Step 7: Confirmation of Booking

Once the booking is successfully processed, confirmation details are generated. Customers receive:

  • Booking reference number (PNR or confirmation code)
  • Flight itinerary details
  • Passenger information

Step 8: Ticket Delivery Timeline

In most cases, booking confirmation is provided after processing. However, confirmation timelines may vary depending on airline systems. In some cases, there may be a delay between payment and final confirmation.

Step 9: Verification with Airline

Customers can verify their booking directly with the airline using the confirmation number provided. This ensures that the reservation is successfully recorded in the airline system.

Step 10: Post-Booking Assistance (Optional)

Customers may contact our support team for assistance with:

  • Flight changes
  • Cancellations
  • Travel-related queries

All changes and cancellations are subject to airline policies and fare rules.
