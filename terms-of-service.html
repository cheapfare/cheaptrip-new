3. Ticketing, Confirmation, Cancellations and Refunds

Fulfilment Policy and Ticket Issuance

A booking request is fulfilled when the relevant ticket is issued and/or the relevant reservation is confirmed and communicated to you, subject to supplier-specific exceptions. A booking acknowledgement, order number, payment receipt, or 'request received' message should not automatically be read as proof that the airline has finally accepted the booking.

24-Hour Handling and Agency Policy

The U.S. Department of Transportation's 24-hour rule for qualifying direct-airline bookings does not automatically apply to tickets purchased through travel agents or online travel agencies. www.cheaptriptoday.com therefore adopts the following position: a penalty-free cancellation is not automatically guaranteed merely because the request falls within 24 hours.

Voluntary Changes and Exchanges

If you ask to change an existing ticket: the airline or validating carrier must permit the change; the new itinerary must have eligible inventory; any fare difference must be paid; any airline exchange/change penalty must be paid where applicable; any cheaptriptoday post-ticket exchange fee may apply; and the reissued ticket may carry new or different fare rules.

Refundable and Non-Refundable Fares

  • Refundable fare: May be eligible for refund to the original payment method, subject to fare rules and cancellation timing.
  • Non-refundable fare: Usually not eligible for cash refund after the applicable cancellation/void period.
  • Restricted/basic fare: May prohibit changes, credits, or refunds depending on fare conditions.

Airline Cancellations, Significant Schedule Changes

Where an airline cancels a flight or makes a qualifying significant schedule change, applicable law and airline policy may provide customers with rebooking or refund rights. Where Noam Flyers Inc. / www.cheaptriptoday.com is the merchant of record for the airfare and applicable law requires a refund, we will process the proper airfare refund when due.

No-Shows, Missed Departures and Missed Connections

A no-show may result in severe consequences. Unless the airline rule, formal waiver, or mandatory law provides otherwise: if you do not travel on the first unused segment and do not notify the airline or www.cheaptriptoday.com before departure, remaining value may be lost; onward or return segments may be cancelled.

Name Corrections, Name Changes and Reissues

www.cheaptriptoday.com distinguishes between:

  • Minor correction: A spelling, formatting, or ordering issue that still refers to the same traveller.
  • Material name change: A change that may effectively attempt to substitute a different traveller.
  • Transfer request: An attempt to transfer a ticket to another person.

Only minor corrections may be possible subject to airline policy and fees.

Ancillary Services, Baggage, Seats and Special Requests

Seat selection, meal preferences, wheelchair assistance, infant linkage, unaccompanied-minor handling, and similar items may be requests rather than guarantees.
